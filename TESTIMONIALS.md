## 🚦 The consent gate — READ BEFORE PUBLISHING ANYTHING

**Baseline (BT policy):** we may always publish a **comment + the person's name only** (Level B) — their own volunteered words, credited to their name, with **no employer/organization named**. This is the floor and needs **no prior ask**. Use it whenever we don't have explicit consent.

**Above the baseline needs explicit consent**, recorded with *date + how*:
- **Naming the client's organization** (Level A full, or Level C company-only) — the organization's reputation is at stake, not just the individual's, so it is the organization's call. Matters *more* for sensitive-sector clients (e.g. a human-rights foundation), not less.
- Photo, logo, or a formal named case study.

Record two fields per entry: **attribution level currently cleared** (defaults to **B**) and, if we asked, **consent status** (date + how). Upgrade the level only when consent is on record.

**Still recommended: send the one-line ask anyway.** Full name + organization is a much stronger promo asset than a bare name, and it costs one sentence in a reply you are often already writing. Name-only is the fallback for when you can't get an answer, not the target.

*Legal note:* a named endorsement processes personal data; name-only + the person's own words is a defensible baseline, and consent is the clean lawful basis for organization attribution.

### Attribution levels (what byline we may use)

| Level | Byline we may print | Consent needed? |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Full** | `Ime Prezime, Titula, Organizacija` | ✅ yes (organization named) |
| **B — Name only** | `Ime Prezime` (+ optional role/segment, no org) | ❌ no — BT baseline |
| **C — Company only** | `Organizacija` (no person) | ✅ yes (organization named) |
| **D — Anonymous** | `Polaznica AI edukacije` / `Voditeljica tima, neprofitna organizacija` | ❌ no |

When unsure whether organization attribution was granted, drop to **B (name only)**. Downgrading is always safe; naming the organization without a recorded yes is not.

## 🗂 How to add an entry

1. Paste the **verbatim** quote (don't paraphrase; keep the person's own words and language).
2. Fill: person, role, company, segment (from the vocab above), date received, source (thread/file).
3. Set **publish consent** = ❌ until asked. When asked and granted, record date + how.
4. Set **attribution level** (A/B/C/D) to what they actually agreed to.
5. Once consent + attribution are both set, add a row to **✅ Approved for public use** with the exact byline, and note which **channels** are OK (Website / LinkedIn / Decks / Proposals / All).
6. If a translation is used publicly, keep the original alongside it and mark which is the source.

**Consent-ask shortcut:** the simplest clean ask is one line in a thank-you reply — e.g. *"Smijem li tvoju rečenicu iskoristiti kao referencu na webu i LinkedInu? Ako da, kako da te potpišem: ime i tvrtka, samo ime, samo tvrtka, ili bez imena?"* — which captures publish consent + attribution level in one go.
